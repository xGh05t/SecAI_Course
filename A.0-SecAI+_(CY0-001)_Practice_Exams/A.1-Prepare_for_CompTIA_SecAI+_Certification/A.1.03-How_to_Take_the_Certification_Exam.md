
# A.1.3 How to Take the Certification Exam

## How Do I Schedule an Exam?
CompTIA exams are scheduled through Pearson VUE. To schedule the exam, you will need an exam voucher. The ***Testing*** page of the CompTIA website provides the latest details on how to schedule an exam.

If you do not have an exam voucher, you will need to purchase one. To purchase a voucher, go to the ***CompTIA store*** and complete the following:

- Find and select your desired certification. This course prepares you for the CompTIA SecAI+ CY0-001 exam.
- Add the appropriate voucher(s) to your shopping cart.
- Checkout.

You will receive information about your voucher. Enter the voucher information when registering for the certification exam.

Where Do I Take an Exam?

When you schedule the exam with Pearson VUE, you will be presented with options to take the exam at a local testing center or at your home or office. If you choose a local testing center, you will be given the option to pick from the testing centers closest to your location. If you choose to test from your home or office, you will be required to meet certain space and computer requirements.

## What Do I Bring to the Exam?
When you schedule your exam, you will receive detailed instructions on what to bring to the testing center. Read those instructions carefully.

Usually, you will need two forms of identification (one with a picture). For example, you could use a driver's license and a credit card. You will typically receive an erasable marker and whiteboard or laminated paper to use during the exam. Notes or other reference material are not allowed inside the testing center. It is recommended that you do not bring personal items to the testing center. However, many testing centers provide a locker to store personal items.

## How is the Exam Administered?
CompTIA certification exams are all computer-based. At the beginning of the exam, you will have an opportunity to view a tutorial on the exam software. Time spent reviewing the tutorial does not count towards the time you have to take the exam.

## How Soon After I Take the Exam Will I Know Whether I've Passed It?
You will receive results as soon as you have completed the exam. The testing program provides immediate feedback and automatically generates a report showing the required passing score and your score. Pick up your exam report before you leave the testing center. You should keep this report in case there are any discrepancies in your certification program.

## If I Don't Pass the Exam, Can I Retake It?
Typically, there is no waiting period before attempting the retake the exam a second time. However, the third and subsequent attempts have a waiting period. See the CompTIA website for specific information on the retake policy.

It's recommended to use your exam results as a reference for further study if you don't pass your exam on the first attempt.
