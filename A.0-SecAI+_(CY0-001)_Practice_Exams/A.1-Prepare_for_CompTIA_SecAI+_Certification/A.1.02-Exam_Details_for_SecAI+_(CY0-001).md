
# A.1.2 Exam Details for SecAI+ (CY0-001)

## Exam Details
| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Number of questions | Maximum of 60 |
| Types of questions | Multiple-choice and performance-based |
| Length of exam | 60 minutes |

## What types of questions are on the exam?
The exam has mostly multiple-choice questions. Additionally, there are a few performance-based questions at the start of the exam and some drag-and-drop activities. The CompTIA website provided sample questions and also information on performance-based questions.

## What's on the Exam?
The questions on certification exams are protected to maintain the integrity of the exam. However, the exam objectives provide a list of topics and tasks that are covered in the exam questions. You should review the exam objectives and make sure you are comfortable with each topic and objective listed.

The exam objectives are broken down into groups called domains. Each domain for this exam makes up a different proportion of the exam questions.

| Domain | Name | Percentage of Exam |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | Basic AI Concepts Related to Cybersecurity | 17% |
| 2.0 | Securing AI Systems | 40% |
| 3.0 | AI-assisted Security | 24% |
| 4.0 | AI Governance, Risk, and Compliance | 19% |

After taking the practice exam, the objectives for the exam are listed on the Report screen. You can use the report to focus your studies to prepare for the exam.

## Use the Practice Materials
This course is intended to help you gain the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to perform the corresponding job roles. The **Practice Exam** is a full practice exam that is the same length and type of questions you will see on the official CompTIA certification exam.

While the practice exam does not include the exact questions as the certification exam, it will help measure your understanding of the topics covered in the CompTIA objectives. After taking the practice exam, the objectives for the exam are listed on the Report screen. You can use the report to focus your studies to prepare for the exam.
