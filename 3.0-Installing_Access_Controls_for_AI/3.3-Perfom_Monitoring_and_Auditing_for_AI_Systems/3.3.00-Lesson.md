
# 3.3 Perform Monitoring and Auditing for AI Systems

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Exam Objectives**
> - 2.5 Given a scenario, implement monitoring and auditing for AI systems.

Monitoring and auditing AI systems can assist in understanding how the system is currently being used and managed. Monitoring may include documenting the number of queries and responses, but may also include the timeline of queries and responses to gauge how well the system is functioning and responding to workloads. We can also identify peak use times that may require modification in the performance of the system. This can ensure that the costs associated with the operation of the AI system are accurate.
