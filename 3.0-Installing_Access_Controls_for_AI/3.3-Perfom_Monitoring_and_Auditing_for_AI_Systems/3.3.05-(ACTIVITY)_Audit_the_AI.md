
## 3.3.5 Activity: Audit the AI

You are a security auditor assigned to review your organization’s AI system for proper monitoring, logging, and regulatory compliance. Recently, there was a suspected security/privacy incident involving the AI system, and leadership wants to confirm whether the logs are sufficient to investigate the event and meet compliance requirements.

Ask the **Ops Log Custodian** for technical logs and operational details. For example:

- Request and review prompt, error, and access logs, focusing on the incident date.
- Examine the logs for gaps, unusual patterns, or missing information (for example, missing entries, unexplained errors, inconsistent timestamps, or visible PII).
- Assess whether appropriate protections are in place for log data, such as encryption, access controls, log sanitization, and remote/backup logging.

Consult the **Log Auditor** to understand and apply relevant internal policies, regulatory requirements, and audit expectations to the evidence you have collected. Decide whether the current logging and monitoring practices are compliant and sufficient to support incident investigation and audit readiness.

When you are ready, submit your audit-style summary to the **Log Auditor**, clearly:

- Describing the main issues you found in the logs
- Explaining the associated compliance or risk impact
- Recommending specific improvements to logging, monitoring, and the protection of log data
