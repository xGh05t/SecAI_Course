
# 3.1 Deploy Access Controls for AI

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Exam Objectives**
> - 2.3 Given a scenario, implement appropriate access controls for AI systems.

Controlling who or what can access your AI systems is fundamental to keeping them secure. There are different access control models you can use—like role-based or attribute-based controls—to tailor permissions based on need. With the evolving threat landscape, it's important to keep a close eye on who's accessing sensitive data, as well as which agents, networks, and APIs are allowed to communicate with your AI. Managing these access points carefully helps reduce risks and keeps both your data and AI operations safe from unauthorized activities.
