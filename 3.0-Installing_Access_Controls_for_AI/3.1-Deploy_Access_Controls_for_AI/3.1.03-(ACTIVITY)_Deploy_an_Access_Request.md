
# 3.1.3 Activity: Deploy an Access Request

You are tasked with reviewing access requests for your organization’s AI systems and have received three new requests:

**Jane (Data Scientist)**: Requests access to training data on Project Atlas for a new research project.

**Mark (Marketing Analyst)**: Requests admin rights on Model Dashboard to review recent model changes.

**Sue (Incident Responder)**: Requests temporary read access to audit logs during investigation of a security alert.

In this activity, you must:
- Decide whether to APPROVE or DENY each request.
- Provide a brief justification for each decision that reflects the organization’s access control policies and risk considerations.
- After addressing all three requests, propose at least one relevant and actionable policy improvement related to how access requests for AI systems are documented, reviewed, approved, or monitored.

How to use the AI roles:

**PolicyBot** – Ask questions about the organization’s access control policies (for example, what qualifies as a “documented business need,” who may receive admin rights, or how temporary access should be handled).

**Access Review Mentor** – When you are ready, share your final approve/deny decisions and justifications for all three requests, and then provide your policy improvement suggestion.

If you need help understanding specific organizational policies at any point, you can ask PolicyBot before finalizing your decisions with the Access Review Mentor.


## PolciyBot
```

For example, you can ask what documentation is required for a “documented business need,” who may receive admin rights, or how temporary access should be handled.

You: Under our organization’s AI access control policies, what qualifies as a documented business need for accessing AI training datasets like those used in Project Atlas?

Are data scientists working on new research projects automatically eligible, or must the project be formally approved and documented first?

PolicyBot: Great questions!

A “documented business need” in our organization typically requires a formal request in the organization’s ticketing or project tracking system, or a signed/approved request from the project lead. Informal email alone is generally insufficient.

For your second question: Data scientists are not automatically eligible for access to AI training datasets, even if they are working on new research projects. The project must first be formally approved and documented, with project lead approval and clear business justification, before access is granted.

If you’d like, I can summarize the policy wording for you or clarify any part of this requirement!

You: According to our AI governance and access control policies, who is authorized to receive admin rights on AI model dashboards?

Are non-technical roles such as marketing analysts allowed admin access, or should they only receive read-only or analyst access?

PolicyBot: According to our policies, only members of the Machine Learning Engineering team with a business justification may be granted admin rights to AI model dashboards.

Non-technical roles, such as marketing analysts, are not permitted to receive admin access. They may be eligible for read-only or analyst-level access depending on their business needs, but admin rights are strictly limited to technical team members with appropriate justification.

Let me know if you would like additional details on access levels or the process for requesting elevated permissions!

You: What are the organization’s policies regarding temporary access to security audit logs during an incident investigation?

Is an incident responder allowed temporary read-only access, and are there requirements such as time limits, logging, or manager approval?

PolicyBot: Our policies state the following regarding temporary access to security audit logs during an incident investigation:

Members of the Incident Response team may receive temporary read access to audit logs specifically to support an active investigation.
There must be a documented incident ticket to justify the access.
Temporary access should be time-bound, meaning it is granted only for the period required to complete the investigation, and must be removed when no longer needed.
While not stated explicitly in the summary, standard procedure also typically involves managerial approval and ensuring that all activity (such as access and review of the logs) is itself auditable.
```

```
A reference to an approved project or incident ticket

The specific access level requested (read-only, analyst, admin)

Manager or project lead approval

Security or technical review for elevated privileges (e.g., admin rights)

Automatic expiration for temporary access (such as incident response log access)

Periodic access reviews (e.g., quarterly audits of AI system permissions)

Comprehensive logging of access grants and usage to ensure actions are auditable
```
