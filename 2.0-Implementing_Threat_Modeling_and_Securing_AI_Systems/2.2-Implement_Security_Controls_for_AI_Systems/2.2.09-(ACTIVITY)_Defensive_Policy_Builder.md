
# 2.2.9 Activity: Defensive Policy Builder

Your company is deploying a new AI-powered HR chatbot that handles sensitive employee data (payroll, personal details, HR requests) and is central to employee services. The chatbot must operate securely and reliably to protect both employees and the organization.

In this activity, you will work with a Defense Policy Mentor to design a layered AI defensive policy for this HR chatbot. For each of these three layers—Model, Gateway, and Testing & Validation—you’ll be prompted to select two controls from the lists below and provide a 1–2 sentence, scenario-specific justification for each choice. If you need definitions or clarification on any control, you can consult ResourceBot.

**Model Controls**: Output Filtering & PII Masking, Role-Based Access Control (Authorization), Input Validation, Prompt Templates (Injection Prevention), Model/API Exposure Limiting

**Gateway Controls**: Prompt Firewall (Centralized Filtering), Rate Limiting, Token Limit Enforcement, File/Input Size Limit, Endpoint Access Controls

**Testing & Validation Controls**: Prompt Injection Testing, Bias Testing, Penetration/Red Team Testing, Data Poisoning / Supply Chain Testing, Guardrail Bypass Simulation
