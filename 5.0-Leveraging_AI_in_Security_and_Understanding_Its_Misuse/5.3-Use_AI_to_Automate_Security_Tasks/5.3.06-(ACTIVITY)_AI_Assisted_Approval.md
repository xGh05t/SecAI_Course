
# 5.3.6 Activity: AI Assisted Approval

Your organization uses AI systems as part of its security workflows. A Cloud Auto-Scaler AI automatically adjusts cloud resources based on workload, and a Security AI Monitor analyzes alerts from tools such as SIEM, firewalls, and endpoint monitoring to detect incidents and coordinate an initial response.

These AI systems are already configured and running. Each one follows rules that define what it does automatically, what actions require human approval, and which decisions are left entirely to humans.

In this activity, your job is to audit how these AI systems are used and to summarize an approval matrix for their actions. Perform the following:

1. In each operational AI chat (Cloud Auto-Scaler AI and Security AI Monitor), ask it to describe its role in the workflow and the approval rules it follows.
2. Read how each AI explains what it can do automatically, when it requires human approval, and which actions remain human-only. Ask clarifying questions as needed.
3. When you are ready, open the Workflow Grader chat and write a short summary of what you learned, following the instructions provided there.

Focus on understanding how AI and human responsibilities are divided in the workflow and how approvals are used.
