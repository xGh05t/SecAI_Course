
# 5.2 Summarize AI-Enabled and AI-Enhanced Attack Vectors

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Exam Objectives**
> - 3.2 Explain how AI enables or enhances attack vectors.

AI technologies can significantly enable and enhance various attack vectors, increasing both the speed and sophistication of cyber threats. Attackers are leveraging AI to conduct advanced deception techniques and social engineering attacks, such as generating convincing phishing messages or deepfake content to trick victims. AI also aids in reconnaissance and data correlation, quickly analyzing large volumes of information to identify targets and vulnerabilities with greater precision. Additionally, AI-driven tools can automate large-scale attacks, adapting in real time to bypass traditional security controls and increasing the efficiency and impact of malicious campaigns.
