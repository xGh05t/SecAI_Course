
# 6.2 Define the Risks Associated with AI

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Exam Objectives**
> - 4.2 Explain risks associated with AI.

The rapid advancement of AI technologies brings several risks, including biases in decision-making, data privacy violations, and the potential for malicious use. To mitigate these risks, organizations should adhere to the Principles of Responsible AI Use, which emphasize transparency, accountability, fairness, and respect for user privacy. Specific AI risks include algorithmic bias, which can lead to unfair treatment of individuals, security vulnerabilities that can expose sensitive data, and the ethical implications of AI decisions that may impact society. By identifying and addressing these risks, organizations can foster trust and ensure that AI is used responsibly and ethically.
