
# 6.2.6 Activity: Conduct a Risk Assessment

Your organization plans to implement a new AI-driven system. You have been asked to conduct a risk assessment, engaging with stakeholders to identify and explain the main risks associated with AI in this context.

Begin by consulting with the Information Security Officer and the AI Risk Stakeholder. Request a brief risk analysis report from each of them. Then, consolidate their findings into a single risk assessment report and share it with the AI Risk Auditor. Use your knowledge of AI risks and responsible AI principles to explain the risks you identify and suggest appropriate mitigations.
