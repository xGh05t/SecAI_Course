
# 6.3 Explain the Impact of Compliance on Business Use and Development of AI

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Exam Objectives**
> - 4.3 Summarize the impact of compliance on business use and development of AI.

The commercial impact of compliance is twofold. On one hand, noncompliance can lead to legal risks, reputational damage, and lost sales. On the other hand, robust compliance can unlock new markets. Demonstrating compliance with established standards and laws can shorten procurement cycles, enable participation in regulated industries, and build trust with clients, governance boards, and regulators.
