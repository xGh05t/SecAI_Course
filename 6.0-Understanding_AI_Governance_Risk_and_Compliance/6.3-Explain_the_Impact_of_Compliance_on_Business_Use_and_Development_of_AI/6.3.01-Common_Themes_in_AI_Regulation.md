
# 6.3.1 Common Themes in AI Regulation

Across these regimes, expectations align with a practical set of actions.

## Establish Accountable Governance
Clearly define who is responsible for AI risk decisions, including oversight from senior leadership, and establish a method for recording these decisions. Ensure that internal policies align with recognized frameworks, which will allow audits to trace requirements back to controls.

## Inventory AI Use Cases and Classify Risk
Maintain an up-to-date register of AI models and tools, detailing their intended use, the data they rely on, and whether they impact safety or rights.

## Conduct Impact and Risk Assessments Before Launch
For high-risk applications, carry out detailed assessments that address data protection, model risk, security threats, fairness and accessibility, and human factors risks. Document testing methods, metrics, and acceptance criteria.

## Design for Lawful Data Use and Human Oversight
Implement principles of data minimization and purpose limitation, ensuring appropriate legal bases for personal data usage. If AI decisions significantly impact individuals, include meaningful human review processes and provide avenues for contesting outcomes.

## Measure and Manage Model Behavior Continuously
Define testing protocols for performance, robustness, and fairness before and after deployment. Establish monitoring processes to detect drift and changes that could adversely affect protected classes or safety. Retain logs necessary to investigate incidents and demonstrate post-market vigilance.

## Ensure Claims Match Reality
Avoid overstating AI capabilities and ensure that all disclosures are accurate. Consumer protection groups may view exaggerated or unsubstantiated AI claims as deceptive. Align marketing materials, documentation, and user interfaces with verified results.

## Manage Third-Party and Open-Source Dependencies
Treat model providers, data brokers, annotation vendors, and toolmakers as regulated suppliers. Require them to provide documentation, security attestations, evidence of bias testing, and commitments to update their offerings.

## Provide Transparency and Resource
Offer clear user notices where required, publish relevant summaries of models and data for high-risk uses, and create channels for users to correct or contest outcomes.
