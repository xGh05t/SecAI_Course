
# 6.3.6 Activity: Analyze an Organization's AI Structure

You have been appointed as an internal compliance consultant. Your task is to analyze your organization’s AI governance structure in preparation for a third-party compliance assessment. By interviewing stakeholders and reviewing provided information, identify strengths, gaps, and risks in the organization’s AI compliance posture, with attention to:

- Governance and accountability structures
- Model inventory/registration
- Data governance and privacy controls
- Risk assessment procedures
- Documentation and audit readiness
- Model fairness and performance management

A practical approach is to start with the **AI Governance & Engineering Lead** to understand overall governance, inventory status, and technical risk management practices, then consult the **Data Privacy Lead** for data governance, consent, minimization, and privacy impact assessments. You may still interact with resources in any order and ask follow-up questions as needed.

When you are ready to submit your analysis, open the **Chief AI Compliance Officer’s** chat to receive summary questions. Your responses to these questions, along with your inquiry process, will be evaluated using the established rubric.
