
# 6.1 Classify Organizational Governance Structures for AI

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Exam Objectives**
> - 4.1.1 > Organizational structures

Organizational governance structures supporting AI are crucial for ensuring effective implementation and management of artificial intelligence initiatives. These structures typically include an AI Center of Excellence (CoE), which serves as a centralized team that provides best practices, resources, and strategic guidance for AI projects across the organization. Additionally, well-defined policies and procedures are essential to establish guidelines on ethical usage, data privacy, and compliance with regulations, enabling teams to navigate the complex landscape of AI responsibly. Furthermore, establishing distinct AI-related roles ensures that diverse expertise is leveraged, fostering collaboration and innovation while mitigating risks associated with AI deployment. Together, these elements create a robust governance framework that supports sustainable and successful AI integration within the organization.
